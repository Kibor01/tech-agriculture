Our Services

KilimoBora

KilimoBora is AgriNova's flagship outreach program dedicated to delivering practical agricultural training directly to farmers. Through mobile training centers and digital learning platforms, we aim to ensure that no farmer is left behind in the transition to modern, sustainable agriculture.

Our mission is to empower smallholder farmers with the knowledge, tools, and market connections they need to thrive in a changing agricultural landscape.

Key Benefits

  • Access to modern farming techniques
  • Better market connections
  • Reduced post-harvest losses
  • Continuous online and offline support

Land Soil Crop Hub

The Land Soil Crop Hub is a practical knowledge center that helps farmers make informed decisions about land use, soil management, and crop production.

Farmers can learn about soil types, fertility management, crop suitability, and sustainable farming techniques through easy-to-understand guides and locally relevant information.

The hub promotes healthier soils, increased productivity, and resilient farming communities.

Market Information

Access real-time and historical market data on agricultural commodities to make informed production and marketing decisions. AgriNova provides price trends, demand analysis, and market information that help farmers assess opportunities and risks.
